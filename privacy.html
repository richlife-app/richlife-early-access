Privacy note

Your money data stays on your machine.

Rich Life is a private app for one household. It runs on a computer you own, and your money data lives there with it. Nothing about your accounts goes to us, or to anyone else.

What Rich Life stores

  • The accounts and cards you connect or import.
  • The transactions from those accounts, and the balances the bank reports.
  • The spending plan you set up: your targets and categories, and the categories you mark as one-time costs.
  • Your notes and edits: renames, splits, rules you teach it, and the card credits you have marked used.

The two of you share one set of this, so you both see the same numbers. There is no hidden copy.

Where it lives

On the machine that runs Rich Life, in a folder on its disk. Usually a Mac mini at home.

  • The app is served only to devices on your own private network. There is no Rich Life server in the cloud.
  • If you connect a bank through Plaid, that connection is between you and Plaid. We never see your bank login.
  • Optional parts, like the local AI helper, run on the same machine and never send anything out.

Who can see it

  • You, and the person you share the household with.
  • Anyone who can use that machine and reach its disk. Keep it somewhere you trust, and set a login on it.
  • Not us. Not advertisers. Not other households. Rich Life has no analytics, no trackers and no third-party scripts. Every page loads only files from the machine itself.

How to delete it

  • Because the data is on your machine, you can remove it yourself at any time. Delete the app's data folder and the household's data is gone.
  • To have us help you delete it, or to ask what is stored, use the same early-access form you used to join, and say what you want removed. We will walk you through it.
  • Deleting the app folder does not close your bank or Plaid connections. Those stay yours to manage at your bank and in Plaid.

Ask through the early-access form on the invite page, and we will answer.
