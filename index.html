Modern money is messy. Keeping track of it shouldn’t be your job.

Pay a card from checking, split dinner on Venmo with a card, move money to savings: the same dollars show up on two or three statements. Add them up and the numbers are wrong, and nobody keeps a plan built on wrong numbers. Rich Life sorts it out for you, then keeps watch.

Two people, one household.

A payment between the two of you is not spending, so the household counts none of it and each of you sees it as “Between you.” Guilt-free purchases on an account that is yours alone show to the other person only as a monthly total and count per category.

Numbers you can trust.

A money app that is wrong once gets double-checked forever. The figures come from code, not from a guess.

Matched, not guessed

Each card payment is matched to the card it paid, each Venmo charge to the account that funded it, and each payment between you to its other side. What you see is counted once.

Your plan, as it ran

Fixed costs, investing, savings and guilt-free spending are measured against the last two finished months.

Your data in

Connect accounts through Plaid, or import statement files. Nothing is typed in by hand.

Tested rules
