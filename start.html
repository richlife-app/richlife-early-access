Getting started

Welcome to early access.

Thanks for trying Rich Life. Early access means you use the real app, on a computer we run, while we are still building it. Some parts will be rough, and a few things will change under you.

That is the point. Your feedback shapes what we fix next. Setup is quick, apart from however long your bank takes to hand over statements.

Install Tailscale

Rich Life lives on a private network, so your phone needs Tailscale first. Install it on your phone, and on your laptop too if you like.

App Store Google Play tailscale.com/download
  • Sign in with any account you already have.
  • We will share the Rich Life computer with your Tailscale account. Open the Tailscale app and accept the share. It shows up as a shared machine.
  • It is a private network. Nothing about Rich Life is on the public internet.

Open your link

The person who invited you sends a one-time sign-up link. It works for 7 days and for one sign-up only.

  • Open the link on your phone, with Tailscale turned on.
  • Make a passkey. You use Face ID, Touch ID or your device PIN. There is no password.
  • Ten recovery codes appear once. Save them in your password manager now. They are the way back in if you lose your phone.

Add your partner or another device

Optional

Open Data, then Devices and members. Tap Invite your partner to bring in the person you share money with, or tap Add a device to sign in on your laptop. Each person makes their own passkey.

Import statements

Bank connections through Plaid are being turned on for early-access households one at a time. Until yours is on, you bring your data in with statement files.

  1. On your bank's website, look for Statements or Activity. Choose Download or Export, and pick CSV if you can. Every bank words this a little differently.
  2. A CSV file is best. A PDF statement works too. Get the last 3 to 6 months for each account and card.
  3. In Rich Life, open the Data screen and tap Add statements. Pick your files.
  4. Check the column guesses once. After that, the next file with the same columns imports in one tap.

Your files are read in your browser. Only the rows are saved on the computer, never the file itself.

Tell it about your pay and set your plan

This part is still being finished, so it may change from one visit to the next. In short, you pick which deposits are your pay, then set your spending plan. If a step feels confusing, that is useful to hear.

Send feedback

In the app, open the menu and tap Send feedback. You can also reply to the message your invite came in.

Three things help most:

  • What you expected.
  • What happened instead.
  • Which screen you were on.

Small things count. A confusing word or an awkward tap is worth a note.

Leaving and your data

You can leave any time. Email privacy@parvislabs.com and we delete your household, including its nightly copies, within 30 days. What we keep and who can see it is in the early-access section of the privacy note.
